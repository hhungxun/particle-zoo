## Era 2: Forces and Phantoms

1. `fermi-beta`
   - Event: `fermi-1934`
   - Diagram: four-fermion beta-decay vertex.
   - Fix needed: move the `G_F` label so it does not cross the fermion lines or vertex.

2. `yukawa`
   - Event: `yukawa-1935`
   - Diagram: nucleon-nucleon interaction mediated by a massive pion/meson exchange.
   - Show the short range as `~ hbar / (m_pi c)`.

3. `muon-track`
   - Event: `muon-1937`
   - Diagram: cosmic-ray cloud-chamber track through absorber material.
   - Should not simply reuse the positron diagram; emphasize penetrating behavior and weak nuclear absorption.

4. `pion-emulsion`
   - Event: `pion-1947`
   - Diagram: photographic emulsion decay chain, `pi -> mu -> e`, with visible kink vertices.
   - Keep labels outside track intersections.

## Era 3: The Particle Zoo

5. `strangeness-associated-production`
   - Events: `strange-particles-1947`, `nishijima-gellmann-1953`
   - Diagram: associated production, for example `pi- + p -> K0 + Lambda0`, followed by delayed V-shaped weak decays.

6. `reines-cowan`
   - Event: `reines-cowan-1956`
   - Diagram: inverse beta detection with prompt positron-annihilation gammas and delayed neutron-capture gammas.

7. `parity-test`
   - Events: `lee-yang-1956`, `wu-1957`
   - Diagram: polarized cobalt-60 nuclei and asymmetric beta emission relative to spin direction.

8. `goldhaber-helicity`
   - Event: `goldhaber-1957`
   - Diagram: electron capture, recoil nucleus, circularly polarized gamma, and inferred left-handed neutrino helicity.

9. `glashow-electroweak`
   - Event: `glashow-1961`
   - Diagram: `SU(2) x U(1)` mixing into photon plus weak bosons, annotated as pre-Higgs/pre-GIM.

## Era 4: Order from Chaos
